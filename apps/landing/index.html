The Problem

Captchas and KYC Both Fail You

Captchas frustrate real humans and still get solved by bots. KYC hands your ID and biometrics to a company you'll never meet, just so it can decide if you're "real." Neither one actually knows you. Aura replaces both with something that does — the people already in your life.

YOU

Your connections — friends, family, coworkers — already know you're real. Aura just gives that knowledge a cryptographic voice.

Verified by People Who Know You

Aura turns the trust that already exists between you and your connections into a portable proof — no strangers, no back-office reviewers, no central authority deciding who counts as real.

Nothing New Is Shared

You never upload an ID or a selfie. Evaluators confirm only what they already know about you — Aura's privacy-preserving proofs keep that confirmation from leaking anything extra, even to Aura itself.

One Attestation, Everywhere

Get verified once and reuse the same proof to log in, claim a grant, vote, or pass any check that needs a real, unique human — no repeating the process for every app.

FAQ

Questions, Answered

Do I need to share my ID or a selfie?

No. Aura never asks for a government ID, a biometric scan, or any document. Verification comes from people who already know you, confirming what they already know.

What is BrightID and why do I need it?

BrightID is the free app that anchors your identity through your social connections. It's the foundation Aura verification is built on — set it up once, and use it everywhere Aura is accepted.

What do my connections actually see or share about me?

Nothing new. Evaluators only confirm things they already know — that you're a real, unique person. Aura's privacy-preserving proofs keep that confirmation from leaking any extra information, even to Aura itself.

Can I use one Aura verification across multiple apps?

Yes. Verification is a portable attestation — get verified once in Aura Verified, then reuse the same proof anywhere that accepts Aura, from logins to governance votes to grant programs.

Is Aura only for crypto apps?

No. Aura is domain-agnostic — it can verify uniqueness, certifications, community membership, and more, making it just as useful for grant programs and communities as it is for on-chain apps.

Is Aura decentralized?

Yes. Evaluation is performed by an open network of independent participants, not a single company, so no one party controls who counts as verified.
