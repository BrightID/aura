Why Aura

Trust, Without a Middleman

Captchas, KYC forms, and centralized moderation all fail the same way: they frustrate real people while letting sophisticated abuse through. Aura replaces them with a web of accountable experts and privacy-preserving attestations.

AURA

Decentralized Verification

No central authority. Experts closest to the source of truth make evaluations, creating a resilient web of trust.

Privacy by Design

Nothing is shared with anyone who doesn't already know it. Verification happens through existing trust relationships.

Digital Attestations

Evaluations produce portable, digital attestations that any product can rely on to bring authenticity to online activity.
